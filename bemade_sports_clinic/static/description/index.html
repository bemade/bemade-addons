Bemade Sports Clinic

Comprehensive Sports Medicine Management System

Overview

The Bemade Sports Clinic module provides a complete solution for managing sports medicine operations, including team management, patient care, injury tracking, and portal access for treatment professionals.

Key Features

  • Team Management - Organize sports teams with coaches and treatment professionals
  • Patient Management - Comprehensive patient records with medical history
  • Injury Tracking - Detailed injury documentation and treatment planning
  • Portal Access - Secure portal for treatment professionals and coaches
  • Activity Management - Task and appointment scheduling system
  • Document Management - Secure document storage and sharing
  • Multi-language Support - Full French Canadian localization

Portal Features

  • Treatment professional portal with team-based access control
  • Coach portal for team and player management
  • Activity creation, completion, and tracking
  • Injury reporting and management
  • Patient information and emergency contacts
  • Secure document upload and download

Security

  • Role-based access control
  • Team-based data isolation
  • Comprehensive audit trails
  • GDPR compliance features
