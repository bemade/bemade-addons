Adds a **Relevant Conversations** tab to the chatter of every record whose
form uses the standard chatter. It lists the conversations explicitly linked
to the record, whatever created the link (Link to Record, the Linked Records
page of a conversation, split, team inbox), newest activity first, with their
status and assignee. A click opens the conversation.

A link is a reference, not a copy: nothing is posted on the record. A record
without linked conversation shows no change. The tab is for internal users
only.
