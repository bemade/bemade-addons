Pin important notes and emails on top of any record's chatter.

Odoo pins messages in Discuss channels only. This module adds a **Pin** / **Unpin**
action to notes and emails in the chatter of every record, for internal users who can
edit the record, and shows a **Pinned (n)** block on top of the chatter that lists them
(even old ones). Pinning is silent: no note, no notification, and the message's
write date is left untouched. Discuss channels keep Odoo's own pin feature.
